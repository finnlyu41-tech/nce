## 不能用普通文本冒充的接口

- `visual-stimulus`：L04, L05, L06, R14-A, R14-GT, W-TA-A, WA-LINE, WA-BAR, WA-PIE, WA-MIXED, WA-MAP, WA-OBJECT。最低接口：Original image/structured visual with question anchors, alt text and usable mobile sizing. Existing per-item answers can remain.
- `table-layout`：L09, R12-A, R12-GT, WA-TABLE。最低接口：Headers/rows/cells with gap-cell question IDs and responsive rendering, or a faithful readable original table asset with anchored input fields.
- `flow-graph`：L10, R13-A, R13-GT, WA-PROCESS。最低接口：Original flow asset plus question anchors, or nodes/edges/conditions/gap-node IDs with accessible rendering; existing answers need not change.
- `multiple-speaker-audio`：L03, L-FORMAT, S-P3。最低接口：Vetted original audio with speaker/turn/evidence timings, or speaker-identified ordered segments with aggregate completion and QA; timeout must suit actual duration.
- `spoken-original-artifact-link`：S-P1, S-P2, S-P3, S-FC, S-LR, S-GRA, S-P, S-FULL。最低接口：Reuse capture/onTakesChange; add a stable attempt-to-original-audio artifact reference, duration and original prompt/turn context. A transcript remains separate from the audio.
- `human-review-evidence`：S-FC, S-LR, S-GRA, S-P, S-FULL, W-TA-A, W-TA-GT, W-TR-A, W-TR-GT, W-CC-A, W-CC-GT, W-LR-A, W-LR-GT, W-GRA-A, W-GRA-GT, W-FORMAT-A, W-FORMAT-GT。最低接口：Append-only review attachment tied to original attempt/artifact with reviewer/time, criterion-specific text/audio evidence and explicitly unassessable dimensions; keep original and revision separate, no invented Band.

原图需稳定题目锚点、可访问替代说明与手机可读性；表格需真实行/列/单元格，流程需真实节点/箭头/分支。音轨需说话人、轮次与证据时点；文本转语音单声部不能证明多人追踪。口语需要原题、原音频与原作答的稳定关联，逐维人工核验保留不可评项；文字稿不能替代发音证据。人工评阅附件只作为最低接口建议，本批不改schema、存储或调度器，不实现音频上传。

当前既有单人听力的多人换轮范围仍缺；听力所有13个本地细分题型中的图形/结构子形式不能由已有3个题型链抹去。表格或图形也不能以把每格改写成普通问句来标完成。Task1视觉/完整书信、Task2完整essay与口语三部分不能由基础短回答替代。评分导读和手动自查不是正式评分，走完地图不等于Band。
