## Remaining linguistic scope considerations

Unrestricted live prompts still naturally permit present perfect progressive variants: `How long have you been living here?`, `I have been living here for five years`, `They have been living here since 2018`, `How long has he been living in London?`, and `He has been living here for ten years`. All preserve the stated continuing residence and the supplied time facts. The general have/has-plus-participle hint in the since-2018 item does not expressly exclude been living. If the target is exclusively lived, the stem should require that structure explicitly. This observation concerns natural English and prompt constraints; it makes no claim about unseen acceptance logic.

The two work duration questions expressly mandate worked, so the otherwise natural been working alternatives were excluded from their variants. That is a clear local boundary rather than faulty wording.

The reading item says the learners have not finished the book, while requiring read this book. `They have not read this book yet` conventionally denies a completed reading and is a suitable constrained elementary answer. It does not explicitly distinguish partial reading from never starting. Permit or mandate finished reading if that distinction becomes the learning objective.

The repair contexts' instruction to retain earlier attempts belongs to workflow history. The response instruction still asks only for the new specified sentence, so no previous answer or correction is appended in this independent solve.

## Novelty assessment

There are 18 tasks in each course, organised into six rounds of three recurring families. Most tasks remain highly constrained sentence construction exercises once the supplied person, vocabulary, adverb, and named structure are read. There are no named-person swaps, but pronoun/person and object substitutions often play an equivalent role.

- **85:** current-completion items mostly vary subject, object, and participle within one frame. The experience family has real distinctions in ever versus never, bare there, and the last just-returned visit. Paris versus London alone adds little structural novelty. Definite-past tasks vary verbs, closed time phrases, and a separable particle but explicitly cue past tense throughout.
- **87:** completion checks mostly repeat one question frame, with worthwhile irregular-participle breadth and have had breakfast. Past follow-ups largely substitute subjects and verbs within When did; the speaker-role repair context adds some perspective practice. The unfinished-state family contains a substantive evidence distinction between a missing completed result and directly observed ongoing effort. The revised final task adds useful synthesis of both facts in two clauses, clearly exceeding the earlier one-clause person swap. It remains scaffolded by the mandated not repair it yet, but, and still try.
- **89:** residence, employment, and presence produce real predicate differences. Contrasting ended and continuing stays helps learners identify whose duration is relevant. Length versus starting point gives a real for/since choice. Five versus ten years, or 2018 versus 2020, is largely substitution practice. The unfinished-action family offers irregular-participle breadth across done, spoken, gone, taken, read, and seen, while completion logic remains repeated.

The revised wording makes the contexts more coherent and improves the last 87 item's transfer value. The bank still principally measures constrained construction, morphology, and retention of supplied facts. Since most stems explicitly name the intended tense or structure, success would be weaker evidence of spontaneous tense choice in an open conversation.
